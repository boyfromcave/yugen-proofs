### What "proven" and "certified" rest on (revision 10.2; docs/what-is-proven.md)

Every figure called proven, and every row labelled certified, rests on the random-oracle model and on the conditions below, some of them OWED, so in the terms of docs/what-is-proven.md it is conditional; every TREE figure (classical and quantum) is in addition conditional on the recursion heuristic C2, read as a joint probability (never a conditional one; equivalence tests do not discharge it), and none is an unconditional theorem. Conditions: (1) C2, the recursion heuristic (the event 'a child proof accepted by the in-circuit verifier and rejected by the native verifier' does not occur; NOT dischargeable by any random-oracle proof; excluded in practice by in-circuit / native verifier equivalence, a tested engineering property; it enters Theorem TE' at the child's acceptance and by keeping the node relation oracle-free); (2) K-A and K-B, OWED: the round-by-round KNOWLEDGE soundness of both IOPs outside the openings; (3) T-1, OWED from the builders: the transcripts' domain separation across systems (H-DS, injective encodings), their fit to Sharp-Grubbs Protocol 6.5, and the reported c' and k_q (until then c' and k_q are estimates, marked E); (3a) C-4, the level and proof-count binding in node statements: met by design, by search, NOT audited (an audit owed); (3b) H-BIND, collision resistance of the concrete statement-digest functions computed inside the node relations (a standard-model assumption, not an oracle property; it excludes SHAPE-VIOLATION and bounds the tree's node count); (4) the modelling assumptions MA-1 (Keccak-256 and SHAKE256 in A, SHA-256 and SHAKE256 in B, as one random oracle with four disjoint parts) and MA-2' (BLAKE3 derive_key, B's leaves and nodes, as that oracle's leaf and node parts); (5) Sharp-Grubbs, ePrint 2026/2615, Theorem 6.6 (2) and Theorem I.16 (1), (2): a PREPRINT; (6) Theorem TE' (docs/lemma-tree-extraction.md revision 3; revisions 1 and 2 refereed, pass with conditions; revision 3, with SHAPE-VIOLATION and Lemma TE.3, UNREFEREED); (7) Lemma QC (the continuation after a database measurement, from Chiesa-Manohar-Spooner, ePrint 2019/834, Lemma 3.2): refereed, pass (docs/qc-and-status-referee.md); (8) Theorem D (system B's repeated openings): refereed, pass with conditions.
